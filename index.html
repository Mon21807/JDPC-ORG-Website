📢 Research Mission Advertisement

Barometer of Cultural and Religious Pluralism in Plateau and Kaduna States, Nigeria

In collaboration with: Observatoire Pharos, JDPC-Jos, and CAFOD

Observatoire Pharos is seeking a researcher with expertise in anthropology, social sciences, ethnology, political science, or related disciplines to work on a study in Plateau and Kaduna States.

About the Consortium:

  • Observatoire Pharos – A French NGO promoting cultural and religious diversity as a strength.
  • JDPC-Jos – Social arm of the Catholic Church in Jos focusing on peace, empowerment, governance, and development.
  • CAFOD – International charity addressing poverty, peacebuilding, and emergency relief in Nigeria.

Methodology:

This research will use the Barometer of Cultural and Religious Pluralism tool to assess identity-related tensions and social cohesion.

Tasks Include:

  • Documentary study and immersion
  • Survey design and team coordination
  • Data collection, transcription, and analysis
  • Report drafting and stakeholder presentation

Timeline:

  • Sept–Oct 2025: Context analysis & training
  • Oct–Nov 2025: Data collection
  • Dec 2025–Mar 2026: Data analysis & report writing
  • Spring 2026: Workshops & dissemination

Profile:

  • PhD or Master’s in a relevant field
  • Strong experience with religious pluralism in Nigeria
  • Knowledge of Plateau and Kaduna States
  • Fluent in English and Hausa

Contract Type: Consultancy (Individual or Institution) | Based in Nigeria | Remote with field missions

Deadline: August 6th, 2025

Send applications to: contact@observatoirepharos.com and jdpcaristasjs@yahoo.com
